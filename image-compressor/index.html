About this tool

This image compressor reduces the file size of photos and graphics directly in your browser. Choose an image, set the quality with the slider and, if you want, a maximum width. The tool shows the original and compressed sizes side by side, so you can find the best balance between size and appearance before downloading.

How to use it

  1. Click the upload area and choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Adjust the quality slider. Lower quality gives a smaller file.
  3. Optionally enter a max width in pixels to scale a large image down.
  4. Compare the sizes, then click Download compressed JPG.

Good to know

Output format: the result is always a JPEG file. Transparent areas in PNG or WebP images become white because JPEG does not support transparency.

Choosing a quality: 70 to 85 percent usually keeps photos looking good while cutting the size substantially. Very low values show visible blocky artifacts.

Resizing: for web use, photos rarely need to be wider than 1600 to 2000 pixels. Reducing dimensions often saves more space than lowering quality alone.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression uses your browser's canvas and runs locally, so your images never leave your device.

Why did my PNG become a JPG?

The tool outputs JPEG, which compresses photos much better. PNG transparency is replaced by a white background.

Will compression reduce image quality?

Yes, JPEG compression is lossy. Moderate settings are usually hard to notice, but you can compare sizes and choose what you accept.

Can the compressed file be larger than the original?

It can happen with images that were already heavily compressed. In that case keep the original or lower the quality or width.
