About this tool

This word counter updates instantly as you type or paste. It shows the number of words, characters with and without spaces, sentences and an estimated reading time. It is useful for essays, social posts, meta descriptions, articles and any text with a length limit.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text into the box.
  2. Read the counts, which update as you type.
  3. Edit your text until it meets your target length.

Good to know

How words are counted: words are groups of characters separated by spaces, tabs or line breaks. Hyphenated words such as well-known count as one word.

Reading time: estimated at 200 words per minute, a common average for adult silent reading. Actual speed varies by reader and content.

Sentences: counted by splitting on periods, exclamation marks and question marks, so abbreviations like Dr. or e.g. may add extra sentences.

Frequently asked questions

Are characters counted with spaces?

The Characters box counts everything including spaces and line breaks. The No spaces box counts only visible characters.

How accurate is the reading time?

It is an estimate based on 200 words per minute. Technical or complex text usually takes longer.

Does it save or upload my text?

No. The counts are calculated in your browser and your text is not sent anywhere.

Does it work for languages without spaces?

Word counts rely on spaces, so for languages such as Chinese or Japanese the character count is more meaningful.
