Password Generator

Create strong random passwords using your browser's secure random generator.

About this tool

This password generator creates random passwords from the character types you select. Choose a length from 8 to 64 characters, switch lowercase, uppercase, numbers and symbols on or off, and click Generate. Randomness comes from your browser's cryptographic generator (crypto.getRandomValues), which is designed for security use, and the password is created on your device. It is never sent or stored.

How to use it

  1. Move the slider to choose a length. Longer is stronger.
  2. Tick the character types you want to include.
  3. Click Generate for a new password.
  4. Click Copy and paste it into your password manager or the sign-up form.

Good to know

How strong is it? Strength depends mainly on length and the size of the character pool. The tool shows an approximate entropy figure in bits. As a rough guide, 16 random characters from all four sets gives well over 90 bits.

Good habits: use a different password for every account, store them in a reputable password manager and turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.

Guaranteed variety: when several character types are selected, the generator includes at least one character from each before shuffling.

Frequently asked questions

Are the passwords really random?

They are generated with the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator, not Math.random, and use rejection sampling to avoid bias.

Does the site see or store my password?

No. Passwords are created locally in your browser and are never transmitted.

How long should my password be?

At least 12 to 16 characters for most accounts, and longer for important ones such as email and banking.

Why can some sites reject symbols?

A few sites limit which symbols are allowed. If so, turn symbols off and increase the length instead.
