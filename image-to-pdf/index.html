About this tool

This tool turns your images into a PDF document. Select one or more JPG, PNG or WebP files and each image is placed on its own A4 page, scaled to fit and centered, in the order you selected them. The PDF is generated in your browser and downloaded to your device.

How to use it

  1. Click the upload area and select one or more images.
  2. Check the file list, which shows the page order.
  3. Click Convert and download PDF.
  4. Open the saved file in any PDF viewer.

Good to know

Page layout: every page is A4 portrait with a 10 mm margin. Images keep their aspect ratio and are never stretched.

Page order: pages follow the order in which your operating system returns the selected files. If the order matters, rename files with numbers, such as 01, 02, 03, before selecting them.

Transparency: transparent PNG areas are filled with white in the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit on the number of images?

There is no fixed limit. Very many large images use a lot of memory in your browser, so convert them in batches if it becomes slow.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The PDF is created locally with JavaScript and your files stay on your device.

Can I change the page size?

Not at the moment. All pages are A4 portrait.

How do I make the PDF smaller?

Compress the images first with the image compressor, then convert them.
