Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Client-side processing

The tools on Nexus Tools Hub run in your web browser using JavaScript. Images, PDFs, text and other content you process are handled on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not store, inspect or share the content you enter into the tools. We do not require an account.

3. Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic request information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operational purposes.

4. Third-party resources

Some pages load open-source libraries or icons from public content delivery networks, such as cdnjs.cloudflare.com, for features like PDF handling, QR codes and Markdown conversion. Your browser contacts those providers directly to download the library files, which means they may see your IP address as part of the request. They do not receive the content you process in the tools. Some browsers may also use online voices for text to speech, which is controlled by the browser.

5. Contact form

If you submit the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to Formspree, which delivers them to us. We use this information only to respond to you.

6. Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use our contact page.
