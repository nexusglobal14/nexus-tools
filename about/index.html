About Nexus Tools Hub

Nexus Tools Hub is a collection of free utilities from Nexus Global Information Technology. We build small, fast tools for everyday jobs such as formatting JSON, compressing images, converting PDFs, generating passwords and creating QR codes.

How we build them

  • Private by design. Tools run in your browser with client-side JavaScript. Files and text you process are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Free and simple. No account, no installation and no paywall.
  • Honest descriptions. Each tool page explains what the tool does, what it does not do and where its limits are.
