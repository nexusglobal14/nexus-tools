Text to Speech

Listen to your text using your device's built-in voices.

About this tool

This tool reads your text aloud using the speech voices already installed on your device or browser. Type or paste text, choose a voice, set the speed and pitch, and press Play. It is handy for proofreading, accessibility, language practice or listening to a draft instead of reading it.

How to use it

  1. Enter or paste the text you want to hear.
  2. Choose a voice from the list. Available voices depend on your device and browser.
  3. Adjust speed and pitch if you like.
  4. Click Play audio. Click Stop to end playback.

Good to know

Voices and languages: the list comes from your operating system and browser, so it differs between Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. Install extra voices in your system settings to get more languages.

Audio files: this tool plays speech through your speakers. It does not create a downloadable audio file.

Proofreading tip: hearing your text read aloud often reveals missing words and awkward sentences that are easy to miss when reading silently.

Frequently asked questions

Does it create an MP3 file?

No. It plays the speech live using your browser's Web Speech API. It does not export audio.

Why is the voice list empty?

Some browsers load voices a moment after the page opens. Wait a second or reload. If it stays empty, your browser may not support speech synthesis.

Is my text sent to a server?

With most browsers the voices run on your device. Some browsers may use online voices, which is controlled by the browser and not by this site.

Can it read other languages?

Yes, if your device has a voice for that language.
